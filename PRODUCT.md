# Aura Quest

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

Flutter app distributed to Android and iOS, including tablet layouts. The
current implementation uses a shared Material component system; the platform
value does not claim distinct Cupertino screens already exist.

## Users

People building routines alone or with friends. A frequent visit is a quick
daily check-in, progress update or response to a quest invitation. This is the
confirmed product workflow, not a researched demographic claim.

## Product Purpose

Make routines into shared quests with visible progress, personal XP, spendable
Aura and playful social accountability. Completing today's activity is the
central task; the game gives it personality.

## Operating Context

Home, Quests, Friends and Profile are the four existing top-level destinations.
Quest details hold progress, participants, activity and Perks. Creating a quest
supports check-in, progress and avoidance goals, schedules, friends, game modes
and balance presets. The app supports Google, Apple and email authentication.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve existing game rules, data flows, navigation and account handling.
- English app text, including the cheeky game messages; German discussion with
  the owner is not a request to translate the product.
- Permanent XP and level are distinct from spendable Aura. Do not promise
  money, prizes or gambling outcomes.
- Both mobile platforms use the same NAS-hosted backend. Docker runs on the NAS.
- Three selectable themes: Brutal Neutralism, Editorial Growth and Auralis.
  New design proposals are scoped exclusively to Editorial Growth. Other
  themes must not inherit its replacement styling.
- Proposed screens may use clearly identified illustrative quest data; do not
  fabricate real usage, popularity, health outcomes or commercial proof.

## Brand Commitments

Aura Quest name and current logo. Playful, provocative English voice with an
adult, professional finish. Editorial Growth is allowed a radically different
visual world, with considered light and dark appearances. Current request is
to compare proposals before selecting a replacement for that theme.

## Evidence on Hand

Current Flutter source, bundled artwork/fonts, existing component screenshot
baselines, Android emulator and internal store test channels. These are product
evidence, not user research or a physical-device performance certificate.

## Product Principles

1. Make the daily action easy to find and fast to complete.
2. Make invitations, progress and consequences understandable.
3. Keep personality in the experience without hiding controls or information.
4. Keep appearance changes independent from game rules and account data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Retain readable contrast, meaningful control labels, enlarged text, platform
safe areas, accessible touch targets and reduced-motion behavior. Tablet and
phone layouts must preserve all capabilities.
