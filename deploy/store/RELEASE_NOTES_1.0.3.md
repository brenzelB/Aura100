# Aura Quest 1.0.3 — Build 24

## Apple App Store — English (en-US)

Your goals. A little more room to grow.

- Meet your weekly or monthly goal, then log optional Bonus days. Your goal stays complete, and extra effort is marked separately in your timeline.
- Give Editorial Growth a fresh look with Cut & Grow, in light and dark mode.
- Create quests in three clear steps and choose Endless quest before setting the dates.
- Spot new activity and invitations more easily.
- Enjoy refined check-in sounds and haptic feedback, with controls in Settings.
- Includes fixes for quest start dates and improvements for smaller screens and larger text.
