# Editorial Growth — design proposals

Three separate visual directions for the selectable theme. No proposal is
implemented in the app. English UI with illustrative data: one of three quests
complete; reading 4/10 pages and movement are still open.

Open `index.html` or serve this directory only on localhost. The comparison
switches every concept between Home/Quest and Light/Dark. The controls inside
each static screen are visual samples, not connected to the NAS.

## Concept derivation

Product mechanism: daily effort becomes personal progress and playful shared
accountability. The use scene is a brief phone visit around the user's routine.
The cultural home includes independent publications, creative communities and
social exercise. Avoid the category's pastel checklist and its predictable
opposite, a glowing game dashboard. Navigation and controls stay familiar.

Grounded systems considered, ordered by resonance:

1. Independent Riso art editions — Cut & Grow; saturated print color, compact
   editorial voice, one geometric progress artwork.
2. Contemporary gallery identities — artwork-scale color and precise labels;
   tasks remain the primary content.
3. Botanical field publications — small growth observations; literal growth is
   limited to this candidate rather than repeated across all seven.
4. Dance rehearsal notation — rhythm and repetition; compact grouped marks.
5. Contemporary running-club publications — Pace Notes; bold workhorse type,
   cobalt/week markers, direct daily actions without fitness-only claims.
6. Color studio sample systems — clear hue roles and repeatable visual states.
7. Independent short-film programs — deliberate framing, short authored motion.

Impeccable reference: `seed:7ba30933aa17fa31`, direction/operate, assigned
grounded candidate 5. The top grounded candidate is shown as a full alternative.
The variable-font-specimen challenger remains a full alternative as Type in
Motion: stronger playful identity, similar product clarity. Other challengers
were assessed for audience fit and task clarity; their verdicts and retained
disciplines are in `.impeccable/editorial-directions.json`. Source grammar is
limited to palette, typography, density and one signature interaction; it does
not replace native navigation or invent new game mechanics.

## Scope and next decision

Only Editorial Growth may adopt a selected direction. Brutal Neutralism and
Auralis must retain their styles. The dark variants are independently composed
palettes. Proposed motion is a next-stage implementation contract, not a claim
that static HTML demonstrates native animation performance. Safe areas, large
text, reduced motion and real native gestures must be checked in Flutter when
a direction is chosen.

The default design path for this native operate screen is code-first. No
standing preference for marketing pages or future projects is recorded.

## Verification boundary

All twelve portrait HTML files were checked for their required views, embedded
fonts, existing four-tab navigation, no scripts and no external dependencies.
These are visual concepts, not native app builds. A Windows Computer Use stop
prevented a final rendered comparison and screenshot capture in this turn;
the concepts have therefore not received a final visual or native review.
No animation or physical-device performance acceptance is implied.
