How it works

Four steps. After that the server keeps score, not your memory.

01

Create a quest

You set the stakes yourself: aura per success, penalty per miss, and how many slips you get before it is over. Then you invite whoever deserves it.

  • Daily, weekly, monthly
  • Rest days allowed
  • Three quest types
02

Log it

One tap a day. On progress quests you collect in parts until the target is full. On avoid quests you only report the slip — otherwise you win by doing nothing.

03

The server settles up

Every hour. It counts check-ins, spends your protective gear, books aura, hands out strikes and ends quests. Even if the app stayed closed all week.

04

Consequences

Out of strikes means out of the quest. Not deleted — you stay in as a spectator and watch the others carry on without you.

Three ways to win a quest

Not every resolution can be ticked off. So there are three.

Check off

One tap and it is done

For anything you either do or you do not. You decide how often per week or month — and which weekdays the quest runs at all. On a rest day it asks for nothing and punishes nothing.

“10 minutes of meditation” · “Gym, 5× a week”

Collect

One target, filled in parts

You pick the amount and the unit. Log several times a day until the target is full — and past it, if you feel like it. Mistyped? Entries can be edited and deleted afterwards.

“100 push-ups” · “2.5 litres of water” · “7,500 steps”

Avoid

Win by doing nothing

Most trackers only know “I did it”. This one also counts what you did not do — and that is half of every resolution people ever make.

You only report the slip. A daily allowance decides how many you can afford: at zero every slip counts immediately, at two you have room. When the period runs out without you blowing the allowance, the server books the win by itself.

“No smoking” (allowance 0) · “Max 2 coffees” (allowance 2)

The part that causes trouble

Aura is not a score.
It is ammunition.

What you earn can be spent on the people you play with. Try it — the buttons work and the prices are the real ones.

Your aura 1450

Plus dice duels: stake aura, two dice, higher total takes the pot. Never more than a quarter of your balance — so a single roll cannot decide the whole quest.

Four modes, four kinds of pressure

The same habit feels completely different in each one.

Free for all

No teams. Everyone scored on their own, all on one board. The way in.

Co-op

One for all. If one person misses a period, the whole party pays the penalty and takes the strike.

Team Battle

Red against blue. At the end the weaker team hands 25 % of its aura to the winners.

Last Standing

Out of strikes, out of the game. Whoever lasts longest collects for every rival they outlived.

And the part that matters:
you cannot cheat

Every rule lives in the database, not in the app. Check-ins, dice, heist odds and settlements are all decided by the server. A tampered phone cannot force anything the database does not allow.
